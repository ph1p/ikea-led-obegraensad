# Troubleshooting

## Serial log

Most problems are easier to find with the serial log. Connect the board over USB and open the
monitor at 115200 baud:

```bash
pio device monitor -b 115200
```

## Display

### The display flickers or shows random pixels

- Check all solder joints and wires between the board and the panel, especially `CLK`, `DI`,
  `LATCH` and `EN`.
- Check that `GND` of the board is connected to `GND` of the panel.
- Use a stable 5 V power supply with enough current.
- Check that the pins in `include/constants.h` match your wiring.

### The board reboots in a loop

The panel can draw more current than the USB port or the board regulator delivers. Power the
board and the panel from a separate 5 V supply, or from one supply that is strong enough for both.
Lower the brightness to test whether power is the cause.

### The display stays dark

- Check the power state: the display can be off while the device runs. Press the button or call
  `PATCH /api/power?state=on`.
- Check the brightness. A [scheduled brightness](/usage/brightness#scheduled-brightness) of `0`
  turns the display dark during its window.

## Network

### The lamp does not connect to Wi-Fi

On ESP32, if the lamp has no stored network, it opens an access point called `IKEA`. Connect to
it and choose your network in the portal. On ESP8266, check the credentials in
`include/secrets.h`. See [Wi-Fi setup](/getting-started/wifi).

- The ESP32 and ESP8266 only support 2.4 GHz networks.
- Move the lamp closer to the router for the first setup.

### I cannot find the IP address

- Open `http://ikea-led.local/`. This needs mDNS on your network.
- Check the client list of your router.
- If MQTT is set up, the IP address is part of the state and the **IP address** sensor in Home
  Assistant.

### The Web GUI does not load

- Check that your computer is on the same network as the lamp.
- Open `http://<device-ip>/` with `http`, not `https`.
- Reload the page after a firmware update. The browser can cache the old GUI.

## Time and messages

### The clock shows the wrong time

Set the time zone and NTP server in [Configuration](/reference/configuration). The lamp needs
internet access to reach the NTP server.

### Messages do not appear

Messages need valid NTP time. Check the clock first. See [Messages](/usage/messages).

## Integrations

### MQTT or Home Assistant does not connect

See [Home Assistant troubleshooting](/integrations/home-assistant#troubleshooting) for connection
states, topics and discovery problems.

### DDP or Art-Net shows nothing

- Select the **DDP** or **Art-Net** plugin first. The lamp only listens while that plugin is
  active.
- DDP needs an ESP32. See [DDP](/integrations/ddp) and [Art-Net](/integrations/artnet).

## Settings are lost after a reboot

ESP8266 builds do not save settings. See [ESP8266 limitations](/getting-started/hardware#esp8266-limitations).

## Still stuck?

Search the [issue tracker](https://github.com/ph1p/ikea-led-obegraensad/issues) or open a new
issue with your board, firmware version and the serial log.
